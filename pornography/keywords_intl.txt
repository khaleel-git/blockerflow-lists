# Blockerflow international adult keywords (Android). 2026-11-05 multilingual design, part 1.
# Format: term|kind|lang. Kinds:
#   strong  : explicit; one match blocks on its own (like keyword_tiers.txt strong)
#   adult   : adult vocabulary; two are needed (or one next to a strong term)
#   context : common word adult sites also use; counts only next to a strong/adult match
#   help    : recovery / help-seeking word; a search with one is not scored (like "quit", "addiction")
#   cancel  : education/health word; cancels adult-tier terms in a search (never a strong one)
# Terms are folded by TextNormalizer (Arabic/Persian variants, ё→е, lowercase) before use.
# Chinese, Japanese, Thai match as substrings (>= 2 CJK / 3 Thai characters); everything else as
# whole words; a Cyrillic or Korean strong term also as the start of a word ("порновидео", "야동을"),
# an Arabic-script one also after a two-letter article ("السكس"). Tuned on Wikipedia titles 2026-11-05.
# Do NOT add a term that is also an ordinary word as strong. Check: Wikipedia title scan (ledger).
